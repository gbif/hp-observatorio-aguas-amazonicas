## Summary

Group of commercial fishes known generically as palometa, which includes species of the genera *Mylossoma*, *Myleus* and *Metynnis*. The most important commercial species are *Mylossoma duriventre* and *Mylossoma aureum*. They are omnivorous fishes, with a preference for fruits and seeds, that form large schools and constitute an important fishery resource in the Amazon.

## Distribution

Potential palometa production is estimated at 7,300 tonnes. The Central Amazon concentrates approximately 75% of recorded catches, followed by the Peruvian Amazon with 13%. They undertake medium-distance migrations (100–1,000 km) associated with Amazonian hydrological cycles.

## Reproduction

The spawning period occurs at the beginning of the rising-water season.

## Feeding

They are omnivorous fishes with a preference for fruits and seeds.

## Habitat

*Mylossoma* species mainly inhabit whitewater and clearwater rivers. They generally do not appear in blackwater rivers, where other paco genera are more abundant. They form large schools and show migratory behavior.

## Uses, management and conservation

Palometas constitute a fishery resource of regional importance in the Amazon. Potential production is estimated at 7,300 tonnes and marketing is oriented mainly toward regional markets within the Amazon basin. They are considered second-category commercial species.
