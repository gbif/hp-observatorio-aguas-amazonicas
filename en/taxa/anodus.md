## Summary

The group of commercial fishes known generically as yulilla comprises several species, among which *Anodus elongatus*, *Hemiodus unimaculatus*, *Hemiodus immaculatus*, *Hemiodus argenteus* and *Hemiodus microlepis* are the most important.

## Distribution

Potential yulilla production is estimated at 1,700 tonnes. Three fishing regions account for 96% of production: the Central Amazon region (44%), the Peruvian Amazon (34%) and Tocantins (19%).

## Reproduction

At the beginning of the rising-water season, they form large schools during spawning and migrate in a dispersed manner.

## Feeding

Yulillas feed mainly on algae. Phytoplanktivorous and algivorous.

## Habitat

They are found in whitewater, clearwater and blackwater rivers.

## Uses, management and conservation

Yulilla is a fishery resource oriented mainly toward regional markets within the Amazon. Its marketing is classified as third-class market status.
