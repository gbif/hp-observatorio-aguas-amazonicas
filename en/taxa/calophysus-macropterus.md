## Summary

The cunshi is a medium-sized omnivorous catfish, with migratory movements of between 100 and 1,000 km. It is caught mainly in the Lower Amazon, where 78% of its production is concentrated, and is important as a commercial fishery resource outside the Amazon. However, information on its biology is limited, especially regarding reproduction and habitat characteristics.

## Distribution

Potential cunshi production is estimated at 980 tonnes. The largest share of catches is concentrated in the Lower Amazon (78%), followed by Tocantins (14%) and the Central Amazon (6%). The species shows a medium-distance migratory pattern, between 100 and 1,000 km.
