## Summary

There are three commercial species in the group of fishes known generically as sardina. *Triportheus elongates* and *T. angulatus* are omnivorous and feed on fleshy fruits and arthropods. *T. albus* also feeds on fruits and arthropods, as well as zooplankton. Sardina species are found in whitewater, blackwater and clearwater rivers. They are migratory and spawn in whitewater rivers.

Potential sardina production is estimated at 3,500 tonnes. The Central Amazon (63%) accounts for the largest share of production, followed by the Peruvian Amazon (24%).

## Feeding

Omnivorous. It feeds mainly on fleshy fruits and arthropods. Some species, such as *Triportheus albus*, complement their diet with zooplankton.

## Habitat

It is found in whitewater, blackwater and clearwater rivers of the Amazon basin.

## Uses, management and conservation

It is a fishery resource of regional importance in the Amazon. It is marketed mainly within the Amazon basin and is classified as a second-class commercial species.
