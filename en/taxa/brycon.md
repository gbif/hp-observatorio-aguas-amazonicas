## Summary

Sábalo comprises migratory and omnivorous species of the genus *Brycon* with potential production estimated at 6,040 tonnes, with fisheries concentrated mainly at the Brazil-Peru-Colombia border. The species show age-differentiated habitat distribution and a marked dietary preference for fruits and seeds of floodplain areas.

## Distribution

Potential sábalo production is estimated at 6,040 tonnes. Fisheries are concentrated in the Brazil-Peru-Colombia Border fishing region, representing 74% of the total, followed by Tocantins (7%), Madeira (6%) and the Peruvian Amazon (3%).

## Reproduction

Both species migrate and are caught mainly during spawning and dispersal migrations. During the low-water season, they often migrate to streams in the lower courses of terra firme creeks, where they remain in pools.

## Feeding

The two sábalo species (*Brycon amazonicus* and *B. cephalus*) are omnivorous when they reach adulthood, although they show a preference for fruits and seeds.

## Habitat

Adults are more abundant in blackwater and in clearwater tributaries, while juveniles remain in whitewater flooded-soil waters.
