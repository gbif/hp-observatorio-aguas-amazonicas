## Summary

Group of commercially exploited fishes that includes several species of the genera *Curimata*, *Potamorhina*, *Psectrogaster* and others. They are detritivorous and migratory fishes, generally small (less than 40 cm in length), that play an important role in Amazonian fisheries. Many fishers consider them one of the largest biomasses of commercial fishes in the Amazon basin because of their abundance and wide distribution.

## Uses, management and conservation

*Curimatidae* species represent one of the most important commercial fish groups of the Amazon, considered by many fishers as one of the largest biomasses available to regional fisheries. Potential production is estimated at approximately 6,200 tonnes and marketing is oriented mainly toward regional Amazonian markets. Within fishing activity they are classified as third-category commercial species.
